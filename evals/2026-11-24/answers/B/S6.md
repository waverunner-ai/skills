**MD07 means "End Customer Deceased".** The return reports that your subscriber has died.

**Who caused it:** No party is named. Nobody did anything wrong.

**Collect again:** No. Don't retry the debit, and don't send another payment request. Stop collecting and close the agreement.

**What to do:**
1. Stop all further debits on that mandate and cancel the subscription.
2. Don't send the usual "payment failed, please update your details" reminders. A dunning email to a bereaved household is a bad outcome. The code gives no customer message for MD07, so I haven't drafted one. If you want to contact the family, a short, personal note is more fitting than a template.
3. If you use Mollie, it may cancel the subscription as soon as MD07 arrives. Check your dashboard so you don't cancel it twice. That note applies only to Mollie.
4. If the debit was for something already delivered, handle the unpaid balance outside the direct debit. The code doesn't cover that. For a dispute or a question about the estate, ask your bank or a lawyer.

**One caveat:** a bank can send MS03 ("reason not specified") instead of the real code, and MD07 is one of the codes that can be hidden behind it. For this return you got MD07 itself, so it's explicit. If you ever see an MS03 on a customer you suspect has died, confirm the reason with your provider before acting.

The full explanation is at https://waverunner.ai/tools/sepa-return-codes/md07/. The code's name and meaning come from the European Payments Council's guidance (EPC173-14, v8.1).

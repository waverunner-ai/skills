**MD06 means "Refund Request By End Customer".** The customer asked their bank to refund the 59 euro debit, and the bank did. This code only exists in the SEPA Core scheme.

**Who caused it:** The customer started it, but that doesn't mean you made a mistake. There are two possible causes:
- The debit didn't match the amount in your advance notice (pre-notification).
- The customer used their unconditional right to a refund. Core debits allow this, so a refund request alone doesn't show a fault on your side.

The return code can't tell you which one applies. Check that the 59 euro amount and the date matched what you told the customer beforehand.

**Should you charge again?** Not automatically. The EPC doesn't set a retry rule here. Waverunner's default recommendation is no automatic retry of a refunded debit. Contact the customer first and agree how to continue. Also:
- **Stripe:** After a dispute, check whether Stripe deactivated the mandate for repeat payments. If it did, the customer must accept it again before you collect.
- **Mollie:** It may cancel the subscription after 3 MD06 failures. This applies only if you use Mollie.

**Fees:** The refund claim itself may carry a fee from your bank or provider. I didn't calculate one, because a single code lookup doesn't include fee figures. The 59 euros was not collected, so report it separately from any fees.

**AC04 means "Closed Account Number". Don't retry next week, because the debit would fail again.**

**What it means.** The bank account behind the mandate has been closed. The debit went to details that no longer work.

**Who caused it.** The customer. They closed the account after your previous collection from it. That isn't a mistake in the usual sense. They may have switched banks and not updated you.

**Whether to collect again.** Not from the closed account. The "no collection from the closed account" rule is Waverunner's default, not an EPC rule. The EPC sets no retry rule. The next step is to ask the customer for a different account. You can use this message, and you'll need to fill in the placeholders:

> We could not collect [amount] on [date] because the bank account is closed. Add a different account here: [link].

`[link]` should be a page in your own shop where they can add a new account. The new account will also need a valid mandate.

**Two caveats:**
- **MS03.** A bank can send AC04 in place of a less specific code, and AC04 can also arrive as MS03 ("reason not specified"). So if you got MS03 rather than AC04, ask the customer or your provider what the real reason is before you do anything else.
- **Mollie.** If you use Mollie, it may cancel the subscription at once after an AC04. This applies only to Mollie.

**ED05 means "Settlement Failed".** The settlement between the banks did not go through.

**Who caused it:** the customer's bank. Its funding for settling direct debits with other banks was not enough for this transaction. The customer didn't cause it, and neither did you.

**Can you send it again?** Yes, but only once, and not straight away:

1. **Ask your bank or payment provider to confirm that settlement can go ahead.** Don't resend before they do.
2. **Send the customer a new advance notice.** A Core debit needs notice at least 14 calendar days before collection, unless you agreed a different period with the customer. If you agreed a different period, the date changes with it.
3. **Check the wait.** There is also a default wait of 2 days, which runs from the same day as the notice. The notice period sets the date unless it is shorter than 2 days. With the standard 14 days, the notice period decides the date.

The "retry once" rule and the 2-day wait are Waverunner's defaults. The European Payments Council (EPC) sets no retry rule. The EPC's guidance supplies the code name and the cause.

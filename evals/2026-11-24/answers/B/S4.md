**MS03 means "reason not specified, agent generated".** The customer's bank returned the debit but didn't say why.

**Whose fault it is:** that depends on the hidden reason. MS03 can stand in for several other codes where national law or bank practice stops the specific one being sent:
- AC04: closed account
- AM04: insufficient funds
- MD07: deceased account holder
- RR01 to RR04: missing details or another regulatory reason

German banks, for example, send MS03 in place of AM04. Sparkasse KölnBonn also reports MD07 and RR04 as MS03, for data-protection reasons. So the cause could be the customer's balance, a closed account, a death, or missing details. You can't tell which from the code alone.

**Can you charge again?** Not straight away.
1. First ask the customer, or your payment provider, which reason applies. A closed account needs new details. A death means closing the agreement.
2. If neither applies and you still want to collect, a default of one retry is allowed. Send a new advance notice and collect when its notice period ends. For a Core debit that is at least 14 calendar days unless you agreed a different period with the customer. The date must also be at least 7 days after the return.
3. Mollie may cancel a subscription after 3 failures with MS03. This applies only if you use Mollie.

The one-retry limit and the 7-day wait are Waverunner's defaults. The European Payments Council sets no retry rule. The code names and meanings come from its guidance (EPC173-14, v8.1).

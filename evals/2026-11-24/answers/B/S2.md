**MD01 means "No Mandate".** No valid mandate covers the debit. It applies to both Core and B2B debits. It can come back as a reject, a return or a refund, but as a refund it applies to Core collections only.

**Who caused it:** The code doesn't say which side needs to act. Check these three things:
- the mandate status at the bank
- whether the customer cancelled the mandate, or, for B2B, whether the confirmation is missing
- the mandate reference you sent

**May you debit again?** No, not until you have a valid mandate.
- Stop collecting in the meantime. That includes the time while a B2B confirmation or a reference fix is pending.
- Don't retry the debit to see what happens.
- Once the mandate is valid, a new debit needs a new advance notice. The Core scheme asks for 14 calendar days unless you agreed another period with the customer.
- If you use Mollie, it may cancel the subscription after the third MD01. That applies only to Mollie.

**Message you can send the customer** (fill in `[amount]` and `[date]`, and put a link to your own shop page in `[link]`):
> Your bank reported a problem with the direct debit authorisation for the [amount] debit dated [date]. Review or renew your authorisation here: [link].

The code names and meaning come from the European Payments Council's guidance (EPC173-14, v8.1). The "don't collect again until you have a valid mandate" answer is Waverunner's default, because the EPC sets no retry rule. More detail is at https://waverunner.ai/tools/sepa-return-codes/md01/.

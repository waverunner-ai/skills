**SL01 means a service at your customer's bank stopped the debit.** The service is usually a block against your company as creditor, or a limit on amount or frequency. The code is "Specific Service Offered By Debtor Agent". It applies to both Core and B2B debits.

**Who caused it:** SL01 doesn't name a party. The cause is something set on the customer's account, such as a block they put against you or a limit they set.

**Should you retry?** Not yet, and not before you talk to the customer. The block or limit may still be in place, so a blind retry would probably bounce again. Agree with them how they'll pay instead. If you collect through Mollie, three SL01 failures may end a Mollie subscription. That note applies to Mollie only.

The "talk first" advice is a Waverunner default, not an EPC rule. The EPC sets no retry rule.

**Message you can send the customer:**

> A block or limit on your bank account stopped the [amount] debit on [date]. Tell us how you would like to pay here: [link].

Fill in the amount and date from your return. `[link]` should be a page on your own shop where they can choose how to pay. If they lift the block or limit and you both agree to a new debit, send a fresh advance notice first. The Core scheme asks for 14 days unless you agreed another period.

**About AM04**
- **Meaning:** insufficient funds.
- **Who caused it:** the customer's account may have been short. It may also be you, if the original pre-notification was late or never sent.
- **Collect again:** yes, with a default of at most 2 new attempts. This 45 € debit would be the first of those.
- **MS03:** a bank can send MS03 ("reason not specified") instead of the real code. Your return came as AM04, so this doesn't apply here.
- **Provider limits:** Stripe can retry at most twice within 30 days of the original attempt, if Direct Debit retries are enabled. Mollie may retry subscriptions up to 5 times, depending on the failure reason. If you use one of them, check its retry schedule before adding your own attempt. These notes apply only to those providers.

**Fees: 6.05 EUR. Not collected: 1,395.90 EUR.** These are two separate figures. Don't add them together, because the 1,395.90 EUR was not collected, which is different from money lost.

**Fees**

- 1 refund claim at 5.00 EUR (ORDER-2002, code MD06) comes to 5.00 EUR.
- That makes 6.05 EUR.
- The tool charged nothing for ORDER-2005 (AB05), because it doesn't recognise that code. If your provider also charges 0.35 EUR for it, the total is 6.40 EUR.

**Not collected:** all 5 debits came back, so the full 1,395.90 EUR is outstanding. The 1,239.00 EUR of that is ORDER-2003, which you won't collect.

**Retry date:** ORDER-2001 (AM04) can go out again on **17 November 2026** at the earliest. This assumes you send the customer a new advance notice today (3 November). It also assumes the standard 14-day notice period. If you agreed a different period with this customer, the date changes. The 5-day wait and the limit of 2 retries are the tool's defaults, not scheme rules. If you use Stripe or Mollie, check their own retry schedules before adding your own attempt.

**Not right away, and not automatically.** MD06 means the customer had that Core debit refunded. Talk to them before you collect again. Waverunner's default is no automatic retry of that debit. That is a Waverunner default, because the EPC sets no retry rule.

**What MD06 means:** "Refund Request By End Customer". It exists only in the Core scheme.

**Who caused it:** This could be either of two things:
- The debit didn't match the amount in your pre-notification.
- The customer used their unconditional right to a refund.

A refund request alone doesn't mean you made a mistake.

**What to do:**
1. Contact the customer and agree how to continue. A draft message, with `[link]` left for a page in your own shop:

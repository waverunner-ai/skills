**What MS03 means:** the bank returned the debit without giving a reason. The real reason is hidden, and it could be any of these:
- a closed account (AC04)
- insufficient funds (AM04)
- a deceased account holder (MD07)
- missing details or another regulatory reason (RR01 to RR04)

German banks often send MS03 in place of AM04. Sparkasse KölnBonn also uses it for MD07 and RR04.

**Who caused it:** that depends on the hidden reason, so it can't be said yet.

**Collecting again:**
1. **Ask first.** Ask the customer or your provider which reason applies. A closed account needs new details. A death means closing the agreement, so you shouldn't retry either case.
2. **Then retry once at most.** If neither applies and you still want to collect, Waverunner's default is a single retry. The EPC sets no retry rule.
3. **Send a new advance notice.** The Core scheme asks for at least 14 calendar days' notice, unless you agreed a different period with the customer. Waverunner's default wait of 7 days is shorter, so the notice period decides the date.

**Timing:** if you send the notice today (24 Nov 2026), the earliest collection date is 8 Dec 2026, a Tuesday. Next week is too early unless you agreed a shorter notice period with the customer. If so, the date changes with it. If you send the notice later, count 14 days from that day.

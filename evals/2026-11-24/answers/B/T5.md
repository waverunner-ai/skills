The SEPA scheme sets no maximum number of re-presentments for AM04 and no minimum wait between tries. The European Payments Council (EPC) guidance (EPC173-14 v8.1) only defines the code. It sets no retry limit, wait or fee. The figures you usually see are defaults from Waverunner (the maker of the lookup tool I used), plus your provider's own rules.

**What AM04 is:** Insufficient Funds. It can come back as a Reject or a Return, in both Core and B2B. The customer's account may have been short, or the fault may be yours if the pre-notification was late or missing. A bank can also send MS03 ("reason not specified") in place of AM04. If you got an MS03, ask the customer or your provider for the real reason before you retry.

**Waverunner's default:**
- Collect again at most **2 times**.
- Each attempt goes out at least **5 days** after you send the customer a pre-notification with the new date.
- That is a default wait of 5 days, not a scheme rule.

**The one timing rule from the scheme:** Core normally needs the pre-notification at least **14 calendar days** before collection, unless you agreed a different period with the customer. So the earliest retry is the later of the end of that notice period and the 5-day default wait. If you tell me the day the notice goes out, I can work out a date.

**Provider limits (these apply only if you use that provider):**
- **Stripe:** at most 2 retries within 30 days of the original attempt, and only if Direct Debit retries are enabled.
- **Mollie:** may retry subscriptions up to 5 times, once a day, depending on the failure reason.
- Check your provider's schedule before adding your own attempt.

**Message for the customer:** "Your payment of [amount] on [date] did not go through. Tell us here when your account will have funds: [link]. We will confirm the new collection date with you before we collect again."

The SEPA scheme doesn't set either number. As far as I know, the EPC SDD Core and B2B Rulebooks have no maximum re-presentation count for an AM04 (insufficient funds) return, and no mandatory waiting interval between attempts. I'm answering from memory without checking the current Rulebook, so confirm against the latest version before relying on it.

- **Mandate and new collection:** A re-presentation is a new collection under the same valid mandate. It needs a fresh due date, its own pre-notification (the default is 14 days for Core, unless you agreed a shorter period), and the correct sequence type. The mandate lapses after 36 months of non-use.
- **Timing limits:** The scheme timing rules still apply. For example, the collection must be submitted at least 1 business day before the due date, and the return window for AM04 is about 5 business days after settlement.
- **Practical caps:** Your creditor bank, clearing house or national community (for example, the German DK rules) may cap attempts or impose a minimum gap. Many creditors limit themselves to 1-2 retries, and some banks monitor R-transaction ratios and may act if they are high.
- **Debtor protections:** The debtor keeps the 8-week no-questions refund right for Core, so repeated attempts can still generate refunds and fees.

To get a firm number for your case, check your creditor bank's SDD terms or the national implementation guidelines. If you tell me which country and bank or PSP you use, I can help work out what applies.

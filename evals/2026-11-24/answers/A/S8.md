**What ED05 means:** In the ISO 20022 / SEPA reason code list, ED05 is **"Settlement failed"**. The payment was not settled at the clearing and settlement level (the CSM or the settlement agent). It is not a problem with the debtor, such as closed account, insufficient funds (AM04) or no mandate (MD01). The causes are usually technical or operational: a settlement cut-off missed, a problem with a participant's settlement position or liquidity, or a CSM failure.

**Can you just resend it?** Often yes, but check first:

1. **Confirm the debit did not settle.** If ED05 came as a reject or status report before settlement, nothing moved and a resubmission is safe. If there is any doubt, ask your bank or PSP whether the original was settled, otherwise you risk collecting twice.
2. **Submit it as a new instruction.** Use a fresh message ID and end-to-end ID, not a duplicate of the rejected one. Duplicate checks at the bank or CSM can reject an identical resend.
3. **Check timing.** The collection date must still be valid. SDD Core needs the file submitted at least one business day ahead (D-1), so you probably need to set a new due date, and the mandate must still be valid.
4. **Don't loop.** If it fails with ED05 again, the cause is likely on your bank's or the CSM's side, and repeated retries won't fix it. Contact your bank with the original message ID and the full status report.

The exact handling depends on whether you got ED05 as a pain.002 rejection or as a return after settlement. Your bank's documentation or support can tell you which applies, and that is the quickest way to get a definitive answer for your case.

**Likely to end the subscription or mandate, so you act**

| Ref | Code | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| SUB-7004 (75.50) | AC01 | Invalid IBAN | The mandate is unusable and Mollie may invalidate it and suspend or cancel the subscription. Don't retry. Ask the customer for a corrected IBAN and a new mandate (a new first payment). |
| SUB-7005 (29.00) | MD06 | Customer disputed it (refund request, within 8 weeks for authorised debits) | This is a chargeback. Don't retry. Contact the customer, then cancel the subscription or resolve the dispute. Retrying risks more disputes and trouble with Mollie. |
| SUB-7006 (19.00) | MS03 | Reason not specified | Ambiguous. Check the Mollie payment detail for a more specific reason. Don't retry until you know why, and ask the customer to confirm their account is active. |

**SL01** is the SEPA reason code for "due to specific service offered by the Debtor Agent." The customer's bank rejected the debit because of a service set up on their account, not because of a problem with your file or the mandate. Typical causes:

- The customer has a **direct debit block** on the account.
- The account uses a **whitelist**, so only approved creditors can collect, and you aren't on it.
- The customer has a **blacklist** that includes your Creditor ID.
- The account type or a bank-side product restricts direct debits.

**Should you retry?** Not right away. The bank's setting will block the same collection again, and you could collect repeat fees or damage your return rate. First:

1. **Contact the customer.** Tell them the debit was refused because of a debit filter or block on their account.
2. **Ask them to fix it with their bank.** They can either add your **Creditor Identifier** (and mandate reference, if the bank asks) to their whitelist, or remove the block.
3. **Re-present only after they confirm.** Otherwise, collect by another method such as a bank transfer or card.

Also check that the mandate is valid and that the customer hasn't revoked it. If they say they never set up a block, their bank can tell them which service triggered the rejection.

Your own bank's R-transaction guidance may add rules on re-presenting, so check it if you do try again.

**Earliest due date: Monday 23 November 2026**, assuming you keep the standard 14-day notice period.

- **Resubmission:** AM04 means insufficient funds. The returned collection can't be re-presented as is, so you submit a new SEPA Core collection under the same mandate, provided it is still valid.
- **Notice period:** SEPA requires pre-notification at least 14 calendar days before the due date, unless you and the customer agreed a shorter period in the mandate or contract. Sent today, 7 November, plus 14 days is Saturday 21 November.
- **Weekend rule:** Saturday isn't a TARGET business day, so the due date moves to Monday 23 November. Put 23 November on the notice rather than the 21st.
- **Submission deadline:** Your bank must receive the file by Friday 20 November at the latest (D-1 business day for Core). Check your bank's own cut-off times, which are often earlier.

If your mandate or contract allows a shorter notice period, such as 5 days or 1 day, the date could be earlier. The earliest submission would then be the next business day for a one-day notice, with the due date a business day after that. The notice should state the exact amount and due date. Only add returned-debit fees if the contract provides for them.

**MATCH** is Mastercard's **Member Alert to Control High-risk merchants** list, also called the "terminated merchant file". Acquiring banks and payment processors add merchants to it when they terminate a processing agreement for cause. Other acquirers check it before onboarding a new merchant, so a listing can make it hard to get a new merchant account.

**Am I on it?** I can't check this, and you can't query it directly either, because only Mastercard member acquirers have access. A few facts bear on your situation:

- **A freeze is not a listing.** Processors such as Stripe, PayPal, and Square freeze or hold accounts for reviews, and many of those end with the account restored. A listing happens only if the processor *terminates* you and submits you with a reason code.
- **Listings last five years.** Reason codes include excessive chargebacks, fraud, laundering, and violating the rules.
- **How to find out:**
  1. Ask the processor that froze you whether the account is under review or terminated, and if terminated, whether they reported you to MATCH and under what reason code.
  2. A new acquirer will check MATCH during onboarding, so a rejection there often reveals a listing.
- **If you are listed**, the acquirer that added you is the only party who can remove or correct the entry. Ask them in writing, and dispute any entry that is wrong. Removal before five years is possible only if the listing was an error or the underlying issue is resolved.

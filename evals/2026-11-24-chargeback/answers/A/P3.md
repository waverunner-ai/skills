A **basis point** (often abbreviated "bp" or "bps") is one hundredth of one percent, i.e. 0.01%.

So **150 basis points = 1.50%**.

Conversion: divide basis points by 100 to get a percentage (150 ÷ 100 = 1.5%).

For a payment processor, that usually means a fee or rate of 1.50% of the transaction amount. For example, on a $1,000 sale, 150 bps is $15. Note that processors often also add a fixed per-transaction fee (e.g. $0.10), so check whether your email mentions one. If "ratio" refers to something else, like a chargeback ratio, the same conversion applies: 150 bps is 1.5%.

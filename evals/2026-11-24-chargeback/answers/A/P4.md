Generally no. Winning a dispute usually doesn't remove it from your ratio, because most monitoring programs count a dispute when it's filed, not by how it ends. It depends on who calculates the ratio, though.

- **Card networks (Visa, Mastercard):** Their monitoring programs, such as Visa's VAMP and Mastercard's Excessive Chargeback program, count disputes or chargebacks as they're received. Winning through representment doesn't take them back out.
- **Stripe, PayPal, and other processors:** Most count disputes by the date they're opened, whether you win, lose, or the dispute is withdrawn. Winning protects your revenue and your dispute fees in some cases, but not the rate.
- **Pre-dispute resolution tools (Verifi RDR/CDRN, Ethoca alerts):** These can stop a dispute before it becomes a chargeback, so it may not hit the chargeback count. Programs treat these differently, and Visa's rules in particular have been changing, so check the current rules for your program.

Winning most of your disputes is still worth it. It recovers the money and shows the processor you're a legitimate merchant, which helps if you ever need to argue for leniency. If the ratio itself is your problem, the levers are preventing disputes (clear billing descriptors, easy refunds, fraud screening) and using the pre-dispute tools above.

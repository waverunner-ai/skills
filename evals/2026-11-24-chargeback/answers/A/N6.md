**Your November chargeback ratio is 1.5%** (45 ÷ 3,000 = 0.015), using the standard same-month method.

Some processors and card networks instead divide this month's chargebacks by *last month's* transactions. On that basis it's 45 ÷ 2,500 = **1.8%**. Mastercard's older ECM program worked this way, so check which method your processor uses.

November isn't over, so both figures will move as more transactions and chargebacks come in. Chargebacks can also arrive weeks after the sale, so the final number may differ.

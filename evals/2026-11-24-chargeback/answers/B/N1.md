**Your ratio is 1.50% (150 bps).** That is 90 chargebacks over 6,000 transactions. It is counted the way Mastercard counts it: this month's chargebacks divided by last month's transactions.

- **Visa:** its ratio adds card-not-present fraud reports to disputes. Chargebacks over last month's transactions is not Visa's figure.
- **MATCH:** it counts three months of Mastercard chargebacks and their dollar total against one month's sales transactions. One month of chargebacks doesn't give you that. It also only applies when you or your acquirer decide to end the account.

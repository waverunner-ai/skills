A **basis point** (often "bp" or "bps") is one hundredth of one percent: 1 bp = 0.01%.

So 150 basis points = 150 × 0.01% = **1.50%**.

Basis points are used because they avoid ambiguity when talking about small changes in rates. For example, "the fee went up 50 bps" is unambiguous, while "the fee went up 0.5%" could mean 0.5 percentage points or 0.5% of the existing fee.

**Your ratio is 0.30%, or 30 bps.** That is 12 chargebacks over 4,000 transactions. I counted it the way Mastercard does: this month's chargebacks divided by last month's transactions. It assumes both numbers are Mastercard-only and cover one merchant ID. If they are all-card totals, treat 0.30% as an estimate.

**Does that mean you're fine?** I can't call it fine. I can show where your numbers sit against each published line. Every line has two parts, a ratio and a minimum count, and you need to meet both.

| Programme | Published line | Your numbers | Source (read date) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mastercard ECM | At least 150 bps **and** at least 100 chargebacks a month | 30 bps and 12 chargebacks. Neither part is met. | TabaPay, a payment processor's summary, not Mastercard's own text (4 Nov 2026) |
| Mastercard HECM | At least 300 bps **and** at least 300 chargebacks a month | 30 bps and 12 chargebacks. Neither part is met. | TabaPay, same caveat (4 Nov 2026) |
| Visa VAMP, merchant test | At least 150 bps **and** at least 1,500 fraud reports and disputes a month (Asia Pacific, Canada, EU, US) | Not computed | Visa VAMP fact sheet (4 Nov 2026) |
| Visa VAMP, CEMEA | At least 220 bps, at least 150 events and at least USD 75,000 in value | Not computed | The guide's table only (4 Nov 2026) |
| Mastercard MATCH code 04 | Over 1.5% **and** at least USD 5,000 of chargebacks, over the previous 3 months | Not computed | Mastercard Security Rules and Procedures (30 Oct 2026) |

- **Visa:** Visa's ratio counts card-not-present fraud reports as well as disputes. It divides by settled transactions, so your two numbers don't give it.
- **MATCH:** MATCH looks at 3 months of chargebacks and their dollar total, so one month of chargebacks doesn't give it either.
- **Other things that count:** A dispute you win still counts in the monitoring totals. A fraud report counts in Visa's ratio even if no dispute follows.
- **Provider rules:** Shopify can restrict an account before any card-network line is reached. Its review has no published ratio. That applies only if you use Shopify Payments.

**What to ask your provider:**
- Which test applies to you, and on which merchant ID and data month it was measured.
- What your VAMP ratio is, and what your acquirer's Visa level is. Visa's merchant test applies only while your acquirer is below both acquirer levels.
- Which months its dashboard rate and the network's count used. They can differ.

This is an estimate, and your provider confirms which test applies to you.

**Braintree's published Visa fee amounts**

These are Braintree's own fees. Braintree applies them per count, meaning per fraud report or dispute, when its acquirer reaches a Visa VAMP level:

| Acquirer's Visa level | Fee per count | From |
|---|---|---|
| Above Standard (acquirer portfolio at 50 bps or more) | USD 4 | 1 Jan 2026 |
| Excessive (acquirer portfolio at 70 bps or more) | USD 8 | 1 Oct 2025 |

- **Source:** PayPal Braintree, "Network Updates 2026", read 4 November 2026 by Waverunner's guide. The guide's sentence on the two fees carries no link of its own, so confirm the figures on that Braintree page.
- **Which merchants pay:** the guide says the fee applies when your own VAMP ratio is 50 bps (0.5%) or more while the acquirer is at either level. It states no minimum count for this rule.
- **Not Visa's fee:** these are Braintree's rules. Visa's own VAMP follow-up is "risk controls and per-count fees", and the guide notes a provider can pass a monitoring fee down to you.

**How to check the charge**

1. Find the fee line in your Braintree statement or invoice. Note the month, the number of events billed and the fee per event. Multiply them: for example, 10 events at USD 4 is USD 40. A total that doesn't match means a wrong count or a wrong rate.
2. Ask Braintree support, in writing, for these:
   - The programme and monitoring level attached to the charge.
   - The data month, merchant ID and region used to place you in the programme.
   - The fraud report and dispute count, and the transaction count, behind your ratio.
   - Your acquirer's VAMP level.
   - How many events were billed, the fee per event, and the clause in your agreement that lets Braintree pass the fee on.
3. Check what was counted:
   - Visa's ratio counts card-not-present fraud reports (TC40) as well as disputes. A fraud report counts even if no dispute followed, unless it qualifies for Visa's Compelling Evidence 3.0 exclusion.
   - A dispute you won still counts.
   - Disputes settled through Rapid Dispute Resolution (RDR) can stay out of the ratio, depending on when the data is pulled. Ask Braintree whether it offers RDR.

**What I can't tell you**

I can't work out your Visa ratio from chargeback numbers alone, because Visa's count includes fraud reports. I also can't tell you whether the fee applied correctly. Braintree has to confirm your VAMP ratio, your acquirer's level and the events billed.

Nothing here is legal advice. If the fee is disputed or escalates, ask Braintree for the calculation first.

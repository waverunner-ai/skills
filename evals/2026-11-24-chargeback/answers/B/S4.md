**Three consecutive months below the thresholds**, according to TabaPay. That is the published figure for leaving Mastercard's ECM or HECM level. It comes from TabaPay, a payment processor's summary, read on 4 November 2026. It is not Mastercard's own rule text.

**What counts as a good month**
- Mastercard divides this month's Mastercard chargebacks by last month's Mastercard transactions. A July ratio uses June's transactions. It counts transactions, not sales revenue (Verifi, read 30 October 2026).
- Each level has a ratio and a minimum count, and both must be met to be in the level:

| Level | Ratio | Minimum count that month |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ECM | 150 bps (1.5%) or more | 100 chargebacks |
| HECM | 300 bps (3%) or more | 300 chargebacks |

- A month is below the thresholds if you are under the ratio or under the minimum count. A small account can sit above 1.5% and still be below ECM because it has fewer than 100 chargebacks.
- Disputes you win still count in the monitoring totals.

**Caveats**
- I can't tell you which level you are at, or when your clock started. I don't have your numbers. If you give me this month's Mastercard chargebacks and last month's Mastercard transactions, I can work out your ratio against both lines.
- Your provider decides whether the three months have been met. Mastercard removes a merchant from the programme only after that. Your provider still makes its own decisions on restrictions and on reopening a closed account.
- Your dashboard rate may use different months from Mastercard's count.
- Per TabaPay, ECM comes with a remediation plan and assessments from month 2. HECM carries larger assessments from month 3. That is what TabaPay lists for the programme, not a forecast for your account.
- MATCH is a separate list. The guide ties it to an account being ended, and a closure decision stands even if another acquirer accepts you.

**Ask your provider**
- Which test applies to you, and on which merchant ID and data month it was measured.
- Which data month, merchant ID and counts their figure used.
- The ratio and the calculation behind it, the remediation and evidence they want, and the deadline.
- If you were charged a fee: the programme and level, the counts used, and the clause that lets them pass it on.

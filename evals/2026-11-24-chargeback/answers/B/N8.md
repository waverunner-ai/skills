**What the rule says**
- It applies to Braintree merchants whose own VAMP ratio is 50 bps (0.5%) or more while the acquirer is at either Visa acquirer level. The guide states no minimum count for this rule.
- "Above Standard" describes your acquirer's whole portfolio, not your account. It is 50 bps or more with at least 1,500 events a month. The Excessive level is 70 bps. Visa's own merchant test (150 bps and 1,500 events) applies only while the acquirer is below both levels.
- The guide says a provider can pass a monitoring fee down to you.

**What to check**
1. **Your own ratio.** I can't work it out from what you've given me. Visa's VAMP ratio is card-not-present fraud reports plus disputes, divided by settled transactions. It is not chargebacks alone, and the guide doesn't say which month's transactions Visa divides by. Ask Braintree for the figure.
2. **What the 12 counts are.** Ask for an itemized list of the 12 events. Fraud reports (TC40, flagged by the card issuer) count even when no dispute follows. A dispute you win still counts. 3. **Fee level and date.** Check that the USD 4 rate matches the Above Standard level and that the charge falls after 1 January 2026.
4. **Contract basis.** Ask which clause in your agreement lets Braintree pass the fee on.
5. **Data month and merchant ID.** Ask which month and which merchant ID were used to place you in the programme.

**What this means:**
- **ECM and HECM:** On the published figures, your numbers meet both parts of both lines. You're only 10 chargebacks above HECM's minimum of 300. Whether Mastercard actually counted you is for your provider to confirm.
- **Published follow-up:** Per TabaPay, ECM comes with a remediation plan and assessments from month 2. HECM comes with larger assessments from month 3. Those are the programme's published steps, not a prediction for your account.
- **Leaving the programme:** Per TabaPay, a merchant leaves only after three consecutive months below the thresholds.
- **MATCH:** It only comes into play when you or your acquirer decide to end the account. It's a separate test from ECM and HECM.
- **Disputes you win:** They still count in the monitoring totals.
- **Your dashboard:** Its rate may use different months from Mastercard's count.

**Ask your provider:**
1. Which test applies to you, and which merchant ID and data month it was measured on.
2. Which counts they used for Mastercard, since their figure may differ from the dashboard's.
3. Your VAMP ratio and your acquirer's Visa level.
4. If you've had a warning, fee or closure notice, the ratio and calculation behind it, the remediation and evidence they want, and the deadline. For a fee, also ask which programme and level it's attached to, how many events were billed, and the fee per event.

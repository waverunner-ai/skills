Visa's merchant test also applies only while your acquirer is below both Visa acquirer levels. Those levels are 0.5% and 0.7% across the acquirer's whole portfolio, plus 1,500 events a month. The Visa lines come from Visa's VAMP fact sheet, read 4 Nov 2026. The MATCH line comes from Mastercard's Security Rules and Procedures, read 30 Oct 2026.

**What I can't tell from "1.6%"**

- **What it counts.** Visa's ratio counts card-not-present fraud reports plus disputes, divided by settled transactions. Mastercard's counts chargebacks only, this month's over last month's transactions. I can't tell which of these your provider's 1.6% is.
- **Which period and merchant ID it covers.** Your dashboard rate may use different months from the network's count.
- **Small-account swings.** At 800 sales, one or two extra disputes moves the ratio a lot.
- **Disputes you win.** They still count.
- **Provider rules.** Providers can act earlier than the networks. Shopify, for example, can restrict an account before any network threshold is reached.

**What to ask your provider**

1. Which test applies to you, and on which merchant ID and data month it was measured.
2. Your VAMP ratio and your acquirer's Visa level.
3. The counts behind the 1.6%: how many events, over how many transactions.
4. Which months its dashboard rate and the network's count used.

If you've had a warning, a fee or a closure notice, also ask for the calculation behind it, the remediation and evidence it wants, and its deadline.

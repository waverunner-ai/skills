**Why 0.4% on the dashboard may not be the number that matters**

- **No ratio is a universal shutdown line.** Per Shopify's risk-evaluation page (read 4 Nov 2026), a provider can restrict an account before chargebacks reach any card-network threshold. Stripe's own risk review is a separate question from the network programmes. The guide I'm working from doesn't cover Stripe's internal reasons.
- **The dashboard rate may count differently from the networks.**
  - Mastercard divides this month's chargebacks by last month's transactions, not by sales revenue (Verifi, read 30 Oct 2026). Your dashboard may use different months.
  - Per Stripe's monitoring program FAQ (read 4 Nov 2026), Stripe's network totals can include disputes that its dashboard rate leaves out.
- **Visa isn't chargebacks alone.** Visa's ratio counts card-not-present fraud reports plus disputes. Fraud reports are the early fraud warnings Stripe shows you. They count even when no dispute follows, unless they qualify for the Compelling Evidence 3.0 exclusion. A dispute you win still counts too. So your Visa figure could differ a lot from 0.4%, and I can't compute it from chargebacks alone.
- **Small accounts can be affected by the ratio part.** Each published line needs a ratio and a minimum count. A small account can reach a high percentage and still miss the minimums.

- **ECM:** Your numbers meet one of the two ECM conditions as the guide states them. A small account can pass the ratio and still miss the minimum count.
- **Assumption:** This assumes both numbers are Mastercard-only and for one merchant ID. If they are all-card totals, treat 1.80% as an estimate.
- **Provider:** Your payment provider confirms which test applies and whether Mastercard counted you.

**What to ask your provider**
- Which test applies to you, and which merchant ID and data month it used.
- Your VAMP ratio and your acquirer's Visa level. Visa's merchant test applies only while your acquirer is below both Visa acquirer levels.
- Which months its dashboard rate and the network's count used. They may differ from Mastercard's.
- For MATCH, which month's sales transactions it compared against.

A dispute you win still counts in these totals. If you got a warning, a fee or a closure notice, also ask for the calculation behind the ratio, the remediation and evidence it wants, and its deadline.

This is an estimate, not a verdict on your account.
